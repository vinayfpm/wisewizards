Tonight's episode

One night in the library

A short cartoon. Every panel ends with something odd but true.

  1. PANEL 1 · MIDNIGHT

    Prof. Know-a-LotStuck at midnight? So was everyone who ever wrote anything.

    Et Al.Hoo else is still up? I'll put the kettle on.

    Odd but true"Et al." is short for the Latin for "and others". Nobody in history has been thanked more and named less.

  2. PANEL 2 · 1 A.M.

    Reviewer TooBoo! Sorry. I meant: lovely paper. Two small questions.

    Prof. Know-a-LotSee? He only haunts drafts he believes in.

    Odd but trueThe first two science journals both began in 1665. People have been waiting on reviewer comments ever since.

  3. PANEL 3 · 3 A.M.

    ScanitisAh… ah… ACHOO! Thirty-four percent!

    Prof. Know-a-LotBless you. That's a number, not a verdict. A person reads it next.

    Odd but trueA similarity score only counts matching text. Whether it is plagiarism is a judgement, and judgements belong to humans.

  4. PANEL 4 · SUNRISE

    Et Al.Sun's up. The draft is better. So are we.

    Prof. Know-a-LotSame time tomorrow? Bring the hard part.

    Odd but trueThe word "scientist" was only coined in the 1830s. Before that you would have been a "natural philosopher", with the same deadlines.

The professor's code

Three spells he refuses to cast

  • No ghostwritingOur only ghost is the reviewer. We improve and guide your work; we do not write it for you.
  • No guaranteesNobody can promise an acceptance, an approval or a grant. Anyone who does is selling fog.
  • No side doorsBuying a service never affects a decision at our journal. Editors judge the paper and nothing else.
