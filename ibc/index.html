Vision and aims

What the journal stands for

Indian Business Chronicles aspires to become the definitive scholarly record of how business works in India: a journal whose research meets the highest standards of international scholarship, and whose insights remain clear enough to guide the classroom, the boardroom and the policy table.

  1. Advance knowledge of Indian businessWork that explains how Indian firms, markets and institutions behave, and how they compare with practice elsewhere.
  2. Treat context as a source of insightStudies in which the Indian or emerging-market setting shapes the findings, and authors explain why.
  3. Unite research and teachingTeaching cases receive the same rigorous review as research articles and appear in issues of their own.
  4. Establish the graphic caseSequential-art cases, each with a peer-reviewed teaching note, as a serious instrument of learning.
  5. Inform practice and policyEvery contribution sets out clear implications for managers, regulators or educators.
  6. Nurture scholarly talentDevelopmental reviews, and a warm welcome for early-career researchers and reflective practitioners.

Aims and scope

Business, management, innovation and technology

India is the journal's principal context; its readership, authorship and editorial board are international. Work on South Asia, other emerging economies and cross-country comparisons is warmly welcomed when it draws out implications for Indian or emerging-market practice. All methods are welcome, provided they suit the question and are applied transparently.

Article types and guidelines

What to send, and in what shape

Every submission includes a blinded manuscript with no author names or affiliations, and a separate title page with each author's name, affiliation and email. Files may be in Word or PDF, up to 20 MB each.

For case authors. Field cases come with a signed release from the organisation, uploaded as a supplementary file; disguised cases are welcome when the disguise is declared to the editor. Cases drawn from published sources cite each source and add insight beyond them. Teaching notes are peer reviewed and released only to verified instructors. Authors of graphic cases hold the rights to all artwork and credit the illustrator as a contributor.

Hallmarks of a strong submission

What the editors look for

Submissions that show these qualities proceed to peer review. Others are returned promptly with a brief explanation, so that authors can redirect their work without delay.

  • A clear questionA business or management question lies at the core of the work.
  • Context that mattersThe manuscript explains why the Indian or emerging-market setting shapes its findings.
  • A genuine contributionThe work advances theory or practice and goes beyond description.
  • OriginalityNeither published nor under review elsewhere, and a complete study rather than a fragment of one.
  • TransparencyAny use of generative AI tools is fully disclosed in the portal's declaration.
  • For casesA genuine decision point, an authentic setting, and the required permissions.

Editorial standards

One standard for every manuscript

Double-blind peer review

Three independent reviewers for every submission. If a reviewer declines, a reserve reviewer is invited at once, so the review proceeds without interruption.

Publication ethics

The journal follows the guidance of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) on authorship, conflicts of interest, research integrity, corrections and retractions.

Originality screening

Every manuscript is screened with Turnitin for textual similarity and AI-generated content, before review and again before publication.

Responsible use of AI

AI tools may support language editing and analysis when declared. They cannot be authors, and authors remain fully accountable. Reviewers do not upload manuscripts to public AI services.

Persistent identifiers

Each article receives a Crossref DOI at Online First publication. Corresponding authors provide an ORCID iD.

Access and charges

A hybrid model: subscription access, with optional open access for authors. Article processing charges are fully waived for Volumes 1 and 2.
